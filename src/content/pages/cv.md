Speech-language pathologist and researcher specializing in swallowing and dysphagia. Currently a Research Officer at the Swallowing Research Laboratory, Faculty of Education, University of Hong Kong.

- **PhD in Speech-Language Pathology (Swallowing)**, The University of Hong Kong, 2025
- **BS Speech Pathology, cum laude**, University of the Philippines Manila, 2017
- Clinical experience in hospital, early-intervention, home-based and telehealth settings
- University teaching at HKU and UP Manila since 2017
